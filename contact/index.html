Report a Problem

Found a tool that isn't working as expected? Use the "Report a problem" button on any tool page, or email us with:

  • The name of the tool.
  • Your browser and device (e.g. Chrome on Windows).
  • A short description of what happened.
  • Steps to reproduce the issue, if possible.

Please do not send us your actual files — we process everything locally and cannot access them.
