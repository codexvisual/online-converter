Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Online Converter is designed around a simple principle: your files are yours. Everything happens in your browser, and we collect as little data as possible. This policy explains what we do and do not collect.

1. File Processing

All files — PDFs, images, documents — are processed locally in your browser. They are never uploaded to any server. Conversion, compression, editing, OCR and optimization run on your device using WebAssembly, Web Workers and Canvas. We do not have access to your files at any point.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your files or their contents.
  • We do not require accounts or personal information.
  • We do not store your documents on any server.
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties (except anonymized ad interactions described below).

3. Information Stored in Your Browser

We use localStorage to remember your theme preference, selected language, and recently used tools. This data stays on your device and is never transmitted. You can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

4. Anonymous Usage Analytics

We may use basic, privacy-friendly analytics (such as page view counts) to understand which tools are popular. This data is aggregated and does not include file contents, filenames, or personal identifiers.

5. Cookies & Advertising

Online Converter may display advertisements, including Google AdSense. Third-party advertisers may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings or the Network Advertising Initiative. See our Cookie Policy for details.

6. Third-Party Libraries

We load open-source libraries from public CDNs (jsDelivr, unpkg) with Subresource Integrity verification. These CDNs may log standard request metadata such as IP addresses according to their own privacy policies.

7. Data Retention

Since we do not store your files, there is nothing to retain or delete on our side. Local preferences remain in your browser until you clear them.

8. Children's Privacy

Online Converter is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. International Users

The service is available worldwide. By using it, you consent to the processing described in this policy in your jurisdiction.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@filofa.com.
