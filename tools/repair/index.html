Repair PDF is a free, browser-based tool that lets you recover data from damaged pdf — without uploading your files anywhere. All processing happens locally in your browser using modern web technologies, so your documents and images stay completely private.

How to Use Repair PDF

  1. Upload the PDF you want to optimize.
  2. Pick a compression level or target size.
  3. Click Process and download the smaller file.

Key Features & Privacy Guarantee

  • Shrink PDF size without visible quality loss.
  • Local processing keeps your documents private.
  • Batch-friendly and instant.
  • Adjust quality vs size trade-off.

Tips & Best Practices

  • Try "Recommended" first, then increase compression if needed.
  • Images dominate PDF size — lower image quality for big savings.
  • Remove unused pages before compressing.

Frequently Asked Questions
