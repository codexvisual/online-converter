Compress IMAGE

Compress IMAGE: Compress JPG, PNG, SVG and GIF images while saving space and maintaining quality. 100% free, private and client-side. No upload required.

Compress IMAGE is a free, browser-based tool that lets you compress jpg, png, svg and gif images while saving space and maintaining quality — without uploading your files anywhere. All processing happens locally in your browser using modern web technologies, so your documents and images stay completely private.

How to Use Compress IMAGE

  1. Upload your image.
  2. Adjust the settings for the chosen tool.
  3. Click Process and download.

Key Features & Privacy Guarantee

  • Edit, convert, compress and optimize images locally.
  • No upload, no tracking.
  • Supports JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP, SVG.
  • Batch processing supported.

Tips & Best Practices

  • Use WebP for web, PNG for transparency, JPG for photos.
  • Keep originals as backups.
  • For print, use 300 DPI or higher.
