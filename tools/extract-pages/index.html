Extract pages

Extract pages: Save selected pages as a new PDF. 100% free, private and client-side. No upload required.

Extract pages is a free, browser-based tool that lets you save selected pages as a new pdf — without uploading your files anywhere. All processing happens locally in your browser using modern web technologies, so your documents and images stay completely private.

Before you begin, gather the files you want to work with and keep an untouched copy of the originals. There is no sign-up and no waiting room: the tool opens instantly and runs on your device. Very large files depend on your computer's available memory, so close unused tabs if a document is unusually big.

How to Use Extract pages

  1. Upload your PDF files using the dropzone or drag-and-drop.
  2. Reorder, select, or set page ranges as needed.
  3. Click Process and download the result.

Key Features & Privacy Guarantee

  • Reorder, merge, split, extract or remove pages in seconds.
  • Works entirely in your browser — files never leave your device.
  • No sign-up, no watermarks, no file size limits.
  • Batch-friendly and instant.

Why Use Extract pages?

A well-organised PDF is faster to read and easier to share. Instead of juggling several separate files you can combine chapters, remove mistakes and put pages in the right order in a single pass.

Common Use Cases

  • Combining a cover page, report and appendix into one document
  • Dropping draft or duplicate pages before sending a contract
  • Rotating scanned pages that arrived the wrong way round

Tips & Best Practices

  • Keep a backup of important PDFs before editing.
  • Use page ranges like "1-3,5,7-9" for precise selection.
  • For large files, close other tabs to free memory.

Review the page order on the preview before saving — it is much quicker than rebuilding the document later.

Privacy & Security

This tool processes your file entirely inside your browser tab. Nothing is uploaded, stored on a server, or shared. The download is generated on your device, so the original file stays private.
