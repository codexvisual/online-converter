Flatten PDF

Flatten PDF: Flatten form fields and annotations into the page. 100% free, private and client-side. No upload required.

Flatten PDF is a free, browser-based tool that lets you flatten form fields and annotations into the page — without uploading your files anywhere. All processing happens locally in your browser using modern web technologies, so your documents and images stay completely private.

How to Use Flatten PDF

  1. Upload your PDF.
  2. Add text, images, watermarks or page numbers.
  3. Click Apply and download.

Key Features & Privacy Guarantee

  • Edit PDFs directly in the browser.
  • Non-destructive, local processing.
  • Free and private.
  • Undo/redo supported.

Tips & Best Practices

  • Save a copy before editing.
  • Use the preview to check placement.
  • Watermarks work best at 30-50% opacity.
