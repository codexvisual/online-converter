PDF Summarize is a free, browser-based tool that lets you get a quick summary of a pdf document — without uploading your files anywhere. All processing happens locally in your browser using modern web technologies, so your documents and images stay completely private.

How to Use PDF Summarize

  1. Upload your PDF.
  2. Run OCR, summarize, translate or redact.
  3. Download the result.

Key Features & Privacy Guarantee

  • Extract text, OCR, summarize, translate and redact.
  • Runs in your browser.
  • Privacy-first.
  • Multiple languages supported.

Tips & Best Practices

  • OCR works best on high-resolution scans.
  • Redact carefully — check all pages.
  • Summaries are extractive; review before sharing.
