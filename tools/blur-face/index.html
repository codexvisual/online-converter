Blur face is a free, browser-based tool that lets you blur faces, licence plates or any selected region to hide private information — without uploading your files anywhere. All processing happens locally in your browser using modern web technologies, so your documents and images stay completely private.

How to Use Blur face

  1. Upload your image.
  2. Adjust the settings for the chosen tool.
  3. Click Process and download.

Key Features & Privacy Guarantee

  • Edit, convert, compress and optimize images locally.
  • No upload, no tracking.
  • Supports JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP, SVG.
  • Batch processing supported.

Tips & Best Practices

  • Use WebP for web, PNG for transparency, JPG for photos.
  • Keep originals as backups.
  • For print, use 300 DPI or higher.
