Header & footer is a free, browser-based tool that lets you add header and footer text to every page — without uploading your files anywhere. All processing happens locally in your browser using modern web technologies, so your documents and images stay completely private.

How to Use Header & footer

  1. Upload your PDF.
  2. Add text, images, watermarks or page numbers.
  3. Click Apply and download.

Key Features & Privacy Guarantee

  • Edit PDFs directly in the browser.
  • Non-destructive, local processing.
  • Free and private.
  • Undo/redo supported.

Tips & Best Practices

  • Save a copy before editing.
  • Use the preview to check placement.
  • Watermarks work best at 30-50% opacity.
