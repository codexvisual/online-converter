Upscale Image

Upscale images with AI Real-ESRGAN 4x or fast resampling. Free, private, client-side — no upload.

Upscale Image enlarges photos while keeping fine detail. Choose the AI engine (Real-ESRGAN 4x, running locally with onnxruntime-web; the ~5 MB model downloads once and is cached in your browser) for reconstructed detail, or the fast balanced mode for instant results. Scale up to 4x and export PNG, JPG or WebP. Your images never leave your device.

Before you begin, gather the files you want to work with and keep an untouched copy of the originals. There is no sign-up and no waiting room: the tool opens instantly and runs on your device. Very large files depend on your computer's available memory, so close unused tabs if a document is unusually big.

How to Use Upscale Image

  1. Upload a JPG, PNG or WebP image.
  2. Choose the AI (Real-ESRGAN) or Fast engine and a scale factor.
  3. Wait for the live preview — on first use the AI model downloads once (~5 MB).
  4. Click Upscale images and download your result.

Key Features & Privacy Guarantee

  • Edit, convert, compress and optimize images locally.
  • No upload, no tracking.
  • Supports JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP, SVG.
  • Batch processing supported.

Why Use Upscale Image?

Images are the heaviest part of most web pages. Compressing, resizing and converting them locally keeps sites fast without uploading personal photos to a third-party server.

Common Use Cases

  • Preparing product photos for an online shop
  • Resizing a screenshot for a presentation
  • Removing a plain background from a profile picture

Tips & Best Practices

  • AI works best on low-resolution photos. Give it a few seconds on the first run.
  • For very large originals use Fast mode — AI analyses up to 384px then resamples to your target size.
  • Export PNG for maximum quality, JPG for smaller files.

Export WebP for the web and PNG when transparency matters.

Privacy & Security

The AI engine downloads an open-source Real-ESRGAN model (~5 MB) once and caches it in your browser; inference then runs locally. Your image itself is never uploaded.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is an AI model downloaded to my device?

Yes. The ~5 MB Real-ESRGAN model is fetched once from Hugging Face and cached in your browser. Your image is never uploaded.

Does upscaling work offline?

Fast mode works offline. AI mode needs the model on first use, then works offline.

What scale factors are available?

2x, 3x and 4x.

Which output formats are supported?

PNG, JPG and WebP.
