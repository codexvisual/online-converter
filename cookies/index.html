Cookie Policy

Last updated: 2026

This policy explains what cookies and similar technologies Online Converter uses, why we use them, and how you can control them.

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that websites store on your device. They allow a site to remember information between visits. Similar technologies include localStorage, which stores data in your browser without sending it to a server.

2. Cookies and Storage We Use

  • Theme preference (localStorage): remembers whether you prefer light, dark or automatic theme.
  • Language preference (localStorage): remembers your selected language (English, Bengali, Spanish, Hindi, Arabic).
  • Recent tools (localStorage): stores a short list of recently used tools for quick access.
  • Studio preferences (localStorage): remembers panel visibility and grid settings in Image Studio.

These are essential for a personalized experience and do not track you across other websites.

3. Third-Party Cookies

Online Converter may display advertisements from third-party networks such as Google AdSense. These networks may set cookies to:

  • Serve ads relevant to your interests.
  • Limit how many times you see the same ad.
  • Measure advertising effectiveness.

We do not control these cookies. You can review Google's practices at Google Ads Technologies.

4. How to Manage Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings:

  • Chrome/Edge: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies.
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies.
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy.

You can also opt out of personalized ads via Google Ads Settings or the NAI opt-out page.

5. Effect of Disabling Cookies

Disabling cookies will not break any tool — all converters work without them. You may lose saved preferences such as theme and language, which will reset to defaults.

6. Data Retention

Local preferences persist until you clear them in your browser. Third-party ad cookies are governed by the advertiser's own retention policies.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Revisions will be posted here with a new date.
