About the Product

Online Converter is a browser-based document and image toolkit. It offers 66 tools — 44 for PDFs and 22 for images — covering the tasks people most often need: merging and splitting PDFs, compressing large files, converting between PDF and Word/Excel/PowerPoint/JPG, protecting and unlocking documents, extracting text with OCR, and editing images in a full non-destructive editor called Image Studio.

What makes the product unusual is its architecture. Every tool runs entirely inside the visitor's browser using JavaScript and WebAssembly libraries loaded from a CDN. Files are never uploaded to a server, which means there is no storage, no retention window and no document database to breach. The site also works offline after the first visit, and is available in English, Bengali, Spanish, Hindi and Arabic with full right-to-left support.

About the Company

Filofa Technology is a software company that builds practical, privacy-respecting web applications. Online Converter is its flagship consumer product. The team focuses on clean design, strong client-side engineering and transparent behaviour: no accounts are required, no watermarks are added, and no file size limits are imposed beyond the memory of the user's own device.
