About Online Converter

Online Converter is a free, privacy-first toolkit for PDF and image tasks. With 66 tools running entirely in your browser, we make everyday document work fast, private and accessible to everyone.

Our Mission

We believe useful document tools should be free, private and simple. Too many "free" online converters upload your files to remote servers, exposing sensitive information. We built Online Converter to prove that powerful document processing can happen entirely on your device — with no uploads, no accounts and no watermarks.

What We Built

Online Converter offers 66 tools across eight categories:

  • Organize PDF: merge, split, extract, remove pages, rotate, organize.
  • Optimize PDF: compress, grayscale, flatten, resize pages.
  • Convert from PDF: PDF to Word, Excel, PowerPoint, JPG, text, CSV, Markdown, HTML.
  • Convert to PDF: images, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, HTML to PDF.
  • Edit PDF: add text, watermarks, page numbers, signatures, crop.
  • PDF Security: protect, unlock, redact, add/remove passwords.
  • PDF Intelligence: OCR, summarize, translate, metadata, compare.
  • Image Tools: compress, resize, crop, convert, upscale, remove background, watermark and more — plus the unified Image Studio editor.

Our Principles

  • Privacy by default: no file uploads, no tracking of your documents.
  • Free forever: all tools are free, unlimited and watermark-free.
  • Transparent: client-side code that anyone can inspect.
  • Accessible: responsive design, dark mode, keyboard navigation and five languages.
  • Offline-capable: works without a connection after the first load.
